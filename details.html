How the recommendation score works

Every dish receives a score out of 100. The highest five scores are shown as alternatives.

Score = 35 x meal-fit + 25 x popularity + 20 x low-waste + 20 x ingredient coverage
35 points - Meal fit

A preferred dish for the selected meal period receives up to 1.5 points after scaling. Historically served-period matches receive an additional half point.

25 points - Popularity

The consumed share is portions consumed divided by portions made. Higher historical consumption earns more points.

20 points - Low waste

Calculated as 1 minus the dish's historical leftover ratio. Lower leftover percentages earn more points.

20 points - Ingredient coverage

Percentage of the dish's listed ingredients available in kitchen inventory. No manual ingredient selection is used on the dashboard.

Carbon information

Carbon estimates use the workbook emission factor for each ingredient: stock or quantity x kg CO2e/kg. The dashboard's carbon hotspot ranking totals purchased kilograms times each ingredient's emission factor.

Turnout and inventory

Predicted turnout uses historical attendance for the selected day and meal period. If there is no exact match, it uses the average attendance for that period. Inventory directly changes ingredient coverage.

Each alternative's amount to make is allocated from predicted turnout in proportion to its recommendation score, so the suggested portions add up to the predicted turnout.
